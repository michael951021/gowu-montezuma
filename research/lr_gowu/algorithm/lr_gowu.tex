\paragraph{Idea.} GoWU \cite{gowu} redistributes particles by \emph{restoring} emulator states
(\proc{Reset}$(p_\text{target}, p_\text{source})$). LR-GoWU keeps GoWU's winner selection, group
consolidation, global synchronization and rollback, but never restores a state: every episode starts at
$s_0 = \cE.\textsc{reset}()$ and a learned goal-conditioned \emph{return policy} $\pi_R$ walks to the selected
landmark. Exploration with GoWU's particle policy starts only after this \emph{handoff}. The search tree of
checkpoints becomes an archive of \emph{landmarks}, one per discretized cell. The archive supplies
hindsight demonstrations that $\pi_R$ imitates. The output is a deployable policy (run $\pi_R$ from reset),
not only a trajectory. The primed numbers mark the counterpart of the paper's algorithm.

\section*{Terms and definitions}
\small
\newcommand{\gap}{\\\addlinespace[5pt]}
\textbf{Objects.}\par\smallskip
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}X@{}}\toprule
$\cell(s)$ & A function from a state to a discrete key, read from the emulator RAM: $\cell(s) = (\text{level}, \text{room}, \#\text{keys held}, \lfloor x/16\rfloor, \lfloor y/16\rfloor)$, where $(x, y)$ is the player's pixel position. States with the same key are treated as the same place.\gap
Landmark $c$ & The record kept for one cell (we name the landmark by its cell key, so ``$\cell(s) = c$'' means $s$ is in landmark $c$'s cell). It stores: $f_c$, an $84{\times}84$ grey frame of the stored state; $R_c$, the episode's cumulative reward at the moment that state was seen; $U_c$, the uncertainty of $f_c$; $u_c$, the parent landmark; $d_c$, the depth ($d_{u_c} + 1$); its demos; and its return statistics. A cell's landmark can be overwritten by a better state but is never deleted.\gap
Archive $\cA$ & The set of all landmarks. The root $c_0 = \cell(s_0)$ is the start cell with $R_{c_0} = 0$. Parent pointers make $\cA$ a tree.\gap
$U(f)$ & A number: the uncertainty of frame $f$, measured as the RND prediction error (squared error between a trained predictor network and a fixed random target network). Frames seen often have low error. One RND is shared by all slots and is trained throughout the run, so a stored $U_c$ goes stale; it is recomputed (``re-scored'') as $U(f_c)$ whenever it is compared.\gap
$\succ^{\text{lex}}$, $\lexargmax$ & As in the paper. $(b,c) \succ^{\text{lex}} (b',c')$ iff $b > b'$, or $b = b'$ and $c > c'$. $\lexargmax_{c \in S}(R_c, U_c)$ returns the landmark in the set $S$ with the highest $R_c$, breaking ties by the highest re-scored $U_c$.\gap
$\Dead$ / $\Alive$ & A step is $\Dead$ if a life is lost, the game is over, or the exploit guard fires (a RAM check for the middle-room key bug; the bug's reward is set to 0). A $\Dead$ step ends the episode. Every other step is $\Alive$.\gap
$\Reach(s, R, g)$ & Returns true or false. $s$ is the current state, $R$ the episode's cumulative reward so far (the sum of rewards since reset, not the one-step reward $r$), and $g$ a landmark. It is true iff $\cell(s) = g$ \emph{and} $R \ge R_g$: the agent stands in $g$'s cell having collected at least the reward that $g$ was recorded with. Being in the right cell with less reward (for example, having skipped a jewel that $g$'s route collected) does not count.\gap
$\Anc_k(g)$ & The landmark reached by following parent pointers $k$ times from $g$ ($\Anc_1(g) = u_g$). If the tree above $g$ has fewer than $k$ levels it returns the root. $\Anc_{\le 32}(g)$ is the list of $g$'s 32 nearest ancestors.\gap
Demo of $c$ & A recorded action sequence that is known to reach $c$ from reset: the prefix $\tau[0{:}t]$ of some episode, cut at the first step $t$ where that episode was in $c$'s cell with $R \ge R_c$. Each landmark keeps its best 4 demos ranked by $R$ (higher first), then length (shorter first). $\pi_R$ imitates them.\gap
Return statistics of $g$ & $W_g$: list of the last ${\le}64$ return attempts to $g$, each $1$ (reached) or $0$ (timed out, stalled or died). $\succr(g)$: mean of $W_g$ (0 if empty). $\ell_g$: running average (EMA, weight $0.1$) of how many steps successful returns took. Counters: $n^{\text{tgt}}_g$, episodes that chose $g$ as goal; $n^{\text{reach}}_g$, returns that reached $g$; $n^{\text{hand}}_g$, handoffs at $g$; $n^{\text{qd}}_g$, handoffs followed by death within 8 steps (``quick death'').\gap
$\Elig(g)$ & Returns true or false: may $g$ be used as a goal? The root always may. Another $g$ is \emph{ineligible} if it looks unreachable ($n^{\text{tgt}}_g \ge 32$, $n^{\text{reach}}_g = 0$ and no demo to learn from), or doomed (at least 16 handoffs and more than 90\% of them died within 8 steps). We also write $\Elig$ for the set of eligible landmarks.\gap
$\proc{Fallback}(g)$ & The first eligible landmark in the list $g, \Anc_1(g), \Anc_2(g), \ldots$; the root if none is.\gap
$\proc{Winner}(m)$ & Group $m$'s current winner: $\lexargmax_{c \in \{g_m\} \cup (I_m \cap \Elig)}(R_c, U_c)$.\gap
Slots and groups & $E = 128$ slots, each running one episode at a time, all stepped in lockstep. They form $M = 4$ groups $G_1, \ldots, G_M$ of $N = 32$ slots ($m(i)$ is slot $i$'s group). Group $m$ keeps a goal $g_m$, the set $I_m$ of landmarks its slots improved since its last consolidation, an episode counter $n_m$ and a consolidation target $K_m$. Slot $i$ keeps $\nu_i$, the goal of its next episode.\gap
$\pi_R$, $V_R$ & The return policy and its value function: one goal-conditioned actor-critic (separate IMPALA encoders). Its input at step $t$ is an image $x_t$ (the last 4 frames, the frame at the last reward, and the goal's frame $f_g$: 6 channels) and a context vector $\kappa_t$ = (32 bits: which of $\Anc_{\le 32}(g)$ this episode has visited; $\mathrm{clip}((R_g - R)/1000, 0, 5)$, the reward still missing; $t/5000$, the time used).\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}X@{}}\toprule
$s, f, r, R, t, \tau$ & Current state, its frame, last one-step reward, cumulative reward since reset, steps since reset, and the episode's trajectory $(s_0, a_1, s_1, \ldots)$.\gap
$\proc{Step}(a)$ & One environment step with all its bookkeeping: $s, r, \textit{dead} \gets \cE.\textsc{step}(a)$; $R \gets R + r$; $t \gets t + 1$; append $(a, s)$ to $\tau$; add $f$ to the RND replay (the frames $U$ is trained on).\gap
$I$, $J$ & $I$: landmarks this episode created or replaced so far. $J$: those created or replaced by the current step (empty or one landmark).\gap
$t_{\text{prog}}$ & Last step at which the episode entered a cell it had not visited before (``progress''). Drives the return stall test and $\beta_t$.\gap
$\beta_t$ & Temperature on $\pi_R$'s logits (an entropy boost when stuck): $\beta_t = 1 + (0.01 \max(0,\, t - t_{\text{prog}} - 100))^2$. It is 1 until 100 steps pass without progress, then grows, flattening the action distribution.\gap
$L$, $\mu L + b$ & Expected return length for $g$ and the return time limit derived from it.\gap
\textit{handed}, $t_h$ & \textit{handed} is true if this episode reached $g$ (or started in it); $t_h$ is the step at which that happened.\gap
$t_0$, $t_{\text{imp}}$ & Step at which the explore phase began; last step at which this episode improved a landmark.\gap
$a_{\text{hold}}$, $h$ & The held random action and the number of steps it is still held. $\cA_{\text{act}}$ is the set of 18 Atari actions.\gap
$r^R_t$ & Reward used to train $\pi_R$ with PPO (return phase only); the game reward $r$ enters it only through $0.1\,\mathrm{sign}(r)$.\\

\end{tabularx}

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{\proc{RunEpisode} (one episode of slot $i$: return to $g$, then explore)}\label{alg:episode}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Input goal landmark $g \in \cA$, return policy $\pi_R$, uncertainty $U$
\Params return time limit $(\mu, b) = (2, 200)$, return stall $500$, explore budget $1000$, explore stall $300$,
\item[] \hspace*{3.6em}episode cap $T_{\max} = 5000$, hold length $[h_{\min}, h_{\max}] = [5, 15]$, $\epsilon = 0.2$, quick death $8$
\State $s \gets \cE.\textsc{reset}()$;\ $R \gets 0$;\ $t \gets 0$;\ $\tau \gets (s)$;\ $I \gets \emptyset$;\ $\textit{handed} \gets \text{false}$;\ $n^{\text{tgt}}_g \gets n^{\text{tgt}}_g + 1$ \Comment{no restore}
\If{$\cell(s) = g$ and $R_g \le 0$}\label{ln:triv} \Comment{goal is the start cell (e.g. root)}
  \State $n^{\text{reach}}_g \gets n^{\text{reach}}_g + 1$;\ $n^{\text{hand}}_g \gets n^{\text{hand}}_g + 1$;\ $\textit{handed} \gets \text{true}$;\ $t_h \gets 0$;\ \textbf{goto} line~\ref{ln:explore}
\EndIf
\LComment{Return phase: replaces Reset$(p_i, v)$ by walking to $g$}
\State $L \gets \ell_g$ if defined, else the length of $g$'s best demo, else $1000$\label{ln:ret}
\Loop \Comment{each pass is one step; $\proc{Step}$ advances $t$}
  \State $a_t \sim \pi_R(\cdot \mid x_t, \kappa_t)$ at temperature $\beta_t$
  \State $\textit{dead} \gets \proc{Step}(a_t)$
  \IfThen{\textbf{not} \textit{dead}}{$I \gets I \cup \proc{UpdateArchive}(s, R, \tau, i)$} \Comment{Algorithm \ref{alg:archive}}
  \State $r^R_t \gets \mathbf{1}[\Reach(s,R,g) \wedge \neg\textit{dead}] + 0.1\cdot\#\{\text{ancestors in } \Anc_{\le 32}(g) \text{ first visited at } t\} + 0.1\,\mathrm{sign}(r)$
  \If{\textit{dead}}
    \State $\proc{RecordReturn}(g, 0)$;\ \Return $(\tau, I, \Dead)$ \Comment{Algorithm \ref{alg:return}}
  \ElsIf{$\Reach(s, R, g)$} \Comment{handoff: reached $g$}
    \State $\proc{RecordReturn}(g, 1)$;\ $\ell_g \gets 0.9\,\ell_g + 0.1\,t$;\ $n^{\text{hand}}_g \gets n^{\text{hand}}_g + 1$;\ $\textit{handed} \gets \text{true}$;\ $t_h \gets t$;\ \textbf{break}
  \ElsIf{$t \ge \mu L + b$ \textbf{or} $t - t_{\text{prog}} \ge 500$ \textbf{or} $t \ge T_{\max}$}
    \State $\proc{RecordReturn}(g, 0)$ \Comment{failed: explore from here anyway}
    \IfThen{$t \ge T_{\max}$}{\Return $(\tau, I, \Alive)$}
    \State \textbf{break}
  \EndIf
\EndLoop\label{ln:retend}
\LComment{Explore phase: GoWU's particle policy (held random action)}
\State $t_0 \gets t$;\ $t_{\text{imp}} \gets t$;\ $h \gets 0$\label{ln:explore} \Comment{$\approx$ a run of Alg.~1 outer steps, l.~4--12}
\Repeat
  \IfThen{$h = 0$}{\label{ln:hold}$a_{\text{hold}} \sim \Unif(\cA_{\text{act}})$;\ $h \sim \Unif\{h_{\min}, \ldots, h_{\max}\}$}
  \State $a_t \gets a_{\text{hold}}$;\ $h \gets h - 1$;\ with probability $\epsilon$: $a_t \sim \Unif(\cA_{\text{act}})$
  \State $\textit{dead} \gets \proc{Step}(a_t)$
  \If{\textit{dead}}
    \IfThen{\textit{handed} \textbf{and} $t - t_h \le 8$}{$n^{\text{qd}}_g \gets n^{\text{qd}}_g + 1$} \Comment{feeds the doomed-landmark gate}
    \State \Return $(\tau, I, \Dead)$
  \EndIf
  \State $J \gets \proc{UpdateArchive}(s, R, \tau, i)$;\ $I \gets I \cup J$;\ \textbf{if} $J \ne \emptyset$ \textbf{then} $t_{\text{imp}} \gets t$
\Until{$t - t_0 \ge 1000$ \textbf{or} $t - t_{\text{imp}} \ge 300$ \textbf{or} $t \ge T_{\max}$}
\State \Return $(\tau, I, \Alive)$\label{ln:end}
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{\proc{UpdateArchive} (every \Alive\ step; replaces $\mathcal{T}.\proc{AddChild}$)}\label{alg:archive}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Input state $s$ (frame $f$), episode reward $R$, trajectory $\tau = \tau[0{:}t]$, slot $i$ in group $m$
\Params $U$ hysteresis $\eta = 0.1$, demos per landmark $4$, demo store cap 12\,GB
\State $c \gets \cell(s)$;\ $c_{\text{prev}} \gets \cell(s_{t-1})$
\IfThen{$c \in \cA$ \textbf{and} $R = R_c$}{$U_c \gets U(f_c)$} \Comment{re-score the incumbent with the current RND}
\If{$c \notin \cA$ \textbf{or} $R > R_c$ \textbf{or} ($R = R_c$ \textbf{and} $U(f) > (1+\eta)\,U_c$)} \Comment{$(R, U) \succ^{\text{lex}} (R_c, U_c)$ with hysteresis}
  \If{$c \notin \cA$}
    \State $u_c \gets c_{\text{prev}}$ if $c_{\text{prev}} \ne c$, else none;\ $d_c \gets d_{u_c} + 1$ (or $0$) \Comment{parent fixed at creation}
  \ElsIf{$R > R_c$}
    \State drop $c$'s demos, $W_c$ and $\ell_c$ \Comment{old routes no longer earn $R_c$}
  \EndIf
  \State $(f_c, R_c, U_c) \gets (f, R, U(f))$;\ $J \gets \{c\}$;\ $I_m \gets I_m \cup \{c\}$ \Comment{$I_m$ grows live, not at episode end}
\Else
  \State $J \gets \emptyset$
\EndIf
\LComment{Hindsight demonstration}
\If{this is the first step of $\tau$ in $c$ with $R \ge R_c$ \textbf{and} $t > 0$}
  \State offer $\tau[0{:}t]$ as a demo of $c$;\ keep the top 4 by ($R$ desc., $t$ asc.) \Comment{only first demos once store is full}
\EndIf
\State \Return $J$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{\proc{RecordReturn} (after every return attempt; failure recovery via rollback)}\label{alg:return}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Input landmark $g$, outcome $o \in \{0, 1\}$
\Params window $w = 64$, success threshold $\rho = 0.05$, rollback range $[k_{\min}, k_{\max}] = [1, 3]$
\State append $o$ to $W_g$ (keep the last $w$);\ \textbf{if} $o = 1$ \textbf{then} $n^{\text{reach}}_g \gets n^{\text{reach}}_g + 1$
\If{$|W_g| = w$ \textbf{and} $\succr(g) < \rho$} \Comment{$\approx$ Alg.~1, l.~14--17}
  \For{each group $m$ with $g_m = g$}
    \State $k \sim \Unif\{k_{\min}, \ldots, k_{\max}\}$;\ $g_m \gets \Anc_k(g)$;\ $\nu_j \gets g_m$ for all $j \in G_m$ \Comment{see Remark 1}
  \EndFor
  \State $W_g \gets \emptyset$ \Comment{cleared even if no group targets $g$}
\EndIf
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{LR-GoWU (replaces GoWU's population loop)}\label{alg:main}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Input environment $\cE$, $E = 128$ slots in $M = 4$ groups
\Params consolidation $[K_{\min}, K_{\max}] = [16, 32]$ episodes, mixing $p_{\text{mix}} = 0.1$, unroll $T = 128$
\State Initialize $\cA \gets \{c_0\}$ with $(f_{c_0}, 0, U(f_{c_0}))$;\ $\pi_R$, $V_R$;\ shared RND $U$ and its frame replay
\State $g_m \gets c_0$, $I_m \gets \emptyset$, $n_m \gets 0$, $K_m \sim \Unif\{K_{\min}..K_{\max}\}$ for all $m$;\ $\nu_i \gets g_{m(i)}$ for all $i$
\Loop \Comment{all $E$ slots step in lockstep}
  \For{$T$ steps}
    \For{each slot $i$ in group $m$ \textbf{in parallel}}
      \If{slot $i$ has no running episode} \Comment{episode start}
        \State $g \gets \nu_i$;\ \textbf{if not} $\Elig(g)$ \textbf{then} $g \gets \proc{Fallback}(g_m)$ \Comment{falls back from the group goal}
        \State start $\proc{RunEpisode}(g)$ in slot $i$ \Comment{Algorithm \ref{alg:episode}}
      \EndIf
      \State advance slot $i$'s episode by one environment step
      \IfThen{the episode is still running}{\textbf{continue}}
      \LComment{Episode end with $(\tau, I, \text{status})$: choose the next goal}
      \If{$\Unif(0,1) < p_{\text{mix}}$}\label{ln:choose}
        \State $\nu_i \sim \{c \in \cA : \Elig(c)\}$ with probability $\propto 1/\sqrt{n^{\text{tgt}}_c + 1}$ \Comment{count-based mixing}
      \ElsIf{status $= \Alive$ \textbf{and} $I \cap \Elig \ne \emptyset$}
        \State $\nu_i \gets \lexargmax_{c \in I \cap \Elig}(R_c, U_c)$ \Comment{survivor continues; $\approx$ Alg.~1, l.~10--11}
      \Else
        \State $\nu_i \gets \proc{Winner}(m)$\label{ln:winner} \Comment{$\approx$ Alg.~1, l.~23}
      \EndIf
      \State $n_m \gets n_m + 1$\label{ln:choose-end}
      \If{$n_m \ge K_m$} \Comment{group consolidation; $\approx$ Alg.~1, l.~29--30}
        \State $g_m \gets \proc{Winner}(m)$;\ $\nu_j \gets g_m$ for all $j \in G_m$ \Comment{see Remark 1}
        \State $I_m \gets \emptyset$;\ $n_m \gets 0$;\ $K_m \sim \Unif\{K_{\min}..K_{\max}\}$
        \State $g^\star \gets \lexargmax_{k \le M}(R_{g_k}, U_{g_k})$ \Comment{global sync; $\approx$ Alg.~2, l.~8--10, 16}
        \For{each group $k$ with $R_{g_k} < R_{g^\star}$}
          \State $g_k \gets g^\star$;\ $\nu_j \gets g^\star$ for all $j \in G_k$
        \EndFor
      \EndIf
    \EndFor
  \EndFor
  \LComment{Learning, once per unroll of $T \cdot E$ = 16{,}384 agent steps}
  \State \textbf{PPO} on the unroll's return-phase steps only, reward $r^R$ (skipped if fewer than 256)
  \State \hspace{1em} terminal at $\Reach$ or \Dead;\ return timeout/stall bootstraps from $\gamma V_R(s_{t+1})$
  \State \textbf{BC}: 8 minibatches of 512 $(x_j, \kappa_j) \to a_j$ from demos: landmark $c \propto 1 - \succr(c)$,
  \State \hspace{1em} skipping $c$ with $|W_c| \ge 8$ and $\succr(c) \ge 0.8$;\ demo uniform among $c$'s;\ $j \sim \Unif\{0..t_{\text{arr}}{-}1\}$
  \State \textbf{Train $U$} (RND predictor): $31$ updates per $1000$ agent steps, batches of $128$ frames from the replay\label{ln:rnd}
\EndLoop
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Remark 1 (consolidation, sync and rollback only partly redirect slots).} A slot's next goal
$\nu_i$ is chosen when its episode ends (Alg.~\ref{alg:main}, l.~\ref{ln:choose}--\ref{ln:choose-end}). The writes ``$\nu_j \gets g_m$''
in consolidation, global sync and rollback therefore last only until slot $j$'s own episode ends, which
overwrites them. They take effect only for the slot that triggered the consolidation. Otherwise these
operations act through $g_m$: it is part of the winner set of every dead slot, and it is the fallback for
ineligible goals. Unlike GoWU (Alg.~1, l.~30), \Alive\ slots that improved a landmark keep following their
own frontier after a consolidation. After a rollback, the winner of a dead slot may still be a high-$R$
landmark from $I_m$ rather than the ancestor.

\paragraph{Remark 2 (where the counters change).} $n^{\text{tgt}}_g$ counts episodes at their start, after
the eligibility fallback. $W_g$ counts every return attempt, including deaths, but not the trivial handoff
of Alg.~\ref{alg:episode}, l.~\ref{ln:triv}. An $R$-improvement of $g$ resets $W_g$, $\ell_g$ and the demos,
but not $n^{\text{tgt}}_g$, $n^{\text{reach}}_g$, $n^{\text{hand}}_g$ or $n^{\text{qd}}_g$.

\section*{Hyperparameters (v2)}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}\toprule
Environment & Montezuma's Revenge, sticky actions 0.25, 18 actions, episode ends at the first life loss, cap 5000 steps\\
Archive & cell $(\text{level}, \text{room}, \#\text{keys}, \lfloor x/16\rfloor, \lfloor y/16\rfloor)$; $U$ hysteresis $0.1$; top-4 demos; 12\,GB demo store\\
RND & Nature torso + 1024-1024 head, input-normalized, lr $3\times10^{-4}$, batch 128, 31 updates / 1k agent steps, replay 512k\\
$\pi_R$, $V_R$ & IMPALA ResNet (16-32-32, 1024-d), separate for $\pi_R$ and $V_R$; heads $1024{+}34 \to 512 \to 18$ / $1$; bf16\\
